# Human-AI Agreement Analysis

## Methodology
A random sample of 20 papers (4 per condition) was drawn from the screened
paper pool across all 5 neurological conditions. The author independently
reviewed each paper's title and abstract, making an Include/Exclude decision,
then compared results against the AI's original screening decisions.

## Results
- **Sample size:** 20 papers (4 per condition)
- **Agreement:** 19/20 (95%)
- **Cohen's Kappa:** 0.857 (almost perfect agreement, Landis & Koch scale)
- **Include decisions (human):** 4/20 (20%)
- **Exclude decisions (human):** 16/20 (80%)

## Disagreement
- **Paper 4** (Mendelian randomization study on cathepsins and
  neuropsychiatric disorders): AI included at CEBM Level 5. Author
  excluded, reasoning that Mendelian randomization establishes
  genetic-causal associations rather than evaluating treatment efficacy.

## Limitation
As the reviewer was also the pipeline developer, an independent
domain-expert reviewer would strengthen future validation.
